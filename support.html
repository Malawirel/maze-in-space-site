Support

Something not working, or a question about the game? Write to us.

Email: etfb.labs@gmail.com

It helps if you tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and the app version (at the bottom of Settings in the game).

Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. You find the same button at the end of the shop. This brings back the Supporter pack on a new or reset iPhone.

Heart packs and tips are used up when you buy them, so the App Store cannot restore them. Your spare hearts travel through iCloud instead (see the next question).

Do my hearts and progress sync between my devices?

Yes, if each device is signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account. Your spare hearts, current run, best levels and tutorial hints appear on the other device, usually within a short time. A heart bought or earned on one device is never lost when the devices sync.

Without iCloud, everything stays on the one iPhone. If you then delete the app, its progress and spare hearts are deleted with it.

I have a new iPhone. Where is my progress?

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account and install the game. Your progress and spare hearts come back from iCloud. Then tap Restore purchases in Settings if you bought the Supporter pack.

How do I change my choices about ads?

Open Settings and tap Privacy choices. This opens Google's form, where you can change what you agreed to. The button appears where Google's consent or opt-out rules apply, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. The game works the same whatever you choose.

A video does not load.

Videos need an internet connection, and sometimes there is simply no video available. The game says "No video right now" and plays on as normal. Try again a little later. You can earn up to 3 hearts from videos per day.

How do I report an inappropriate ad?

Every video ad shows Google's AdChoices icon in a corner. Tap it to open Google's "About this ad" page, which has a way to report the ad. You can also write to us and tell us what you saw.

A purchase went wrong, or I want a refund.

Apple handles payments and refunds. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If hearts you bought did not arrive, first close and reopen the game: a purchase that was interrupted is credited at the next start. If that does not help, write to us.

A purchase says "Waiting for approval".

This happens when Ask to Buy is on for the Apple Account, for example in a Family Sharing group. Once the purchase is approved, the game credits it, even if the game was closed in between.

How do I start over?

Open Settings and tap Reset progress. This clears your run, your best levels and the tutorial hints. Your purchases and spare hearts stay.

What data does the game collect?

The game has no account and does not track you. Your progress stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. The optional video ads come from Google AdMob, which collects some data to deliver them. The privacy policy has the details.
