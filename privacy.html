Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

The short version

  • You do not need an account. The game has no sign-in.
  • The app does not track you and never asks for permission to track.
  • We run no server. Your progress stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We never receive it.
  • Video ads come from Google AdMob. A video only plays when you tap to watch one. Google collects some data to deliver these ads, as described below.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your payment details.

Data on your iPhone and in your iCloud

To save your game, the app stores the following on your iPhone:

  • your progress: the current run, your best levels and which tutorial hints you have seen;
  • your settings: music, sound and haptics;
  • your spare hearts: a record of hearts earned, spent and bought, including the transaction numbers of your purchases, so that each purchase is credited once;
  • a random device number, created by the app and kept in this iPhone's keychain. It only keeps the heart counts of your devices apart when they are merged. It is not linked to your name, and the app does not use it for ads.

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a copy of this data in iCloud key-value storage, so your progress and hearts appear on your other devices with the same Apple Account. This copy lives in your own iCloud account. Apple stores it under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot see it, and it is never sent to us.

Deleting your data. "Reset progress" in Settings clears your run, best levels and hints, on this iPhone and in the iCloud copy. Your purchases and spare hearts stay, so you do not lose what you paid for.

  • On the iPhone: deleting the app removes its saved game and settings from that iPhone. The random device number in the keychain is kept by iOS after the app is deleted and is used again if you install the app again; it is removed when the iPhone is erased (Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Erase All Content and Settings). It contains no information about you.
  • In iCloud: deleting the app does not delete the copy in your iCloud; it stays there so that a new install or your other devices can pick up your game and hearts. It is removed when you delete the app's data or your iCloud data through Apple, for example in your iPhone's iCloud storage settings (Settings → your name → iCloud) or by deleting your Apple Account. We have no access to this copy, so we cannot delete it for you.

Purchases

Heart packs, the Supporter pack and tips are in-app purchases handled by Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The app receives a confirmation of the purchase from Apple (such as which product and a transaction number) and uses it only to credit your hearts or unlock the Supporter rocket. "Restore purchases" asks Apple for your purchases again. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Video ads from Google AdMob

The game offers optional video ads, for example for a free heart or a revive. They come from Google AdMob (Google's Mobile Ads SDK). There are no banner ads and no ads that play on their own.

Shortly after the app starts, it checks with Google whether a consent message is needed where you are. Where ads may be requested, the app loads one video in advance, so it is ready when you tap. Nothing is shown until you tap to watch.

According to Google's own disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK, Google may collect:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device;
  • a device identifier. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only available to apps you allow to track you. This app never asks, so it is not available;
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them, such as video views;
  • performance and diagnostic data, such as app launch time and crash logs.

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud and abuse. We ask Google not to personalize ads in this app, and to show only ads suitable for general audiences (Google's "G" rating). Google says that ads which are not personalized still use the current context, including a rough location such as the city, and may use identifiers to limit how often you see an ad and for aggregated reports.

Google collects this data as an independent controller and handles it under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The app also lists ad networks for Apple's SKAdNetwork. This lets Apple tell an advertiser that an ad led to an install, without identifying you.

Your consent and choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before any ad is requested. It appears the first time you tap to watch a video, never at launch. In some US states, Google's message lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.

You can change your choices at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy choices. This button is shown where Google's consent or opt-out rules apply.

Whatever you choose, the game works the same. Watching videos is always optional.

No tracking, no analytics, no accounts

The app does not track you across apps or websites, does not ask for permission to track, and does not use the advertising identifier. It contains no analytics tools of its own and has no accounts or sign-in.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous statistics such as crash reports and usage numbers through App Store Connect.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to answer you and delete them once your request is settled, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. Our mailbox is a Gmail account provided by Google, so your email is stored by Google (see International transfers).

Children

Maze in Space is a game for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not in the App Store's Kids category. Ads in the app are limited to content for general audiences.

Legal bases

If the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies to you, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Saving your game, settings, spare hearts and the device number on your iPhone and in your iCloud: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, because this is needed to provide the game and the purchases you asked for. Storing this data on your iPhone is strictly necessary for that service (§ 25(2) no. 2 of the German TDDDG).
  • Crediting purchases: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (the purchase contract).
  • Video ads in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: your consent in Google's consent form, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy choices; this does not affect processing before the withdrawal.
  • Video ads outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, where Google asks for no consent: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in financing a free game with optional, non-personalized ads.
  • Answering your email: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where it concerns the game or a purchase, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in answering questions.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to ask for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent at any time (in the app: Settings → Privacy choices). You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live or the one responsible for us: Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen (Hannover, Germany).

We do not hold your game data, so we cannot look it up or delete it for you: it is on your iPhone and in your iCloud. For data that Google collects for ads, see Google's Privacy Policy for how to use your rights with Google. For anything else, write to us.
