A sliding puzzle in space

Swipe to launch your rocket. It slides until something stops it. Find the way to the planet without flying off the grid.

Every level is new and generated on your iPhone, and each one has a solution. Portals, satellites and switches join in as you go. The game is free. Video ads are optional and only play when you tap to watch one. Extra hearts and a supporter pack are available as in-app purchases.
